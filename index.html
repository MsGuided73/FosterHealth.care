Innovative Technology & AI Solutions for Healthcare

Foster Health builds AI-driven healthcare technologies that help people and organizations better understand health information, support informed decision-making, and plan healthier futures.

Our platforms are designed to support—not replace—human expertise by transforming complex healthcare data into clear, actionable insights.

Our Solutions

For Patients

We empower patients with intelligent tools that help them better understand and engage with their health information.

From helping patients better understand medical reports and lab results to providing clarity around diagnoses, medications, and treatment options, Foster Health supports individuals in navigating healthcare with greater confidence—so they can take a more active role in their care in collaboration with their healthcare providers.

For Providers

Foster Health supports physicians and healthcare professionals with AI-enabled tools that streamline workflows and support informed clinical decision-making.

By synthesizing patient data and surfacing relevant insights, our solutions help providers improve patient interactions, optimize practice operations, and deliver more informed and efficient care—without adding administrative burden.

For Employers & Organizations

We help employers, organizations, and public-sector entities better understand and manage population health.

Our AI-powered platforms provide actionable insights into workforce and community health trends—helping organizations support wellbeing initiatives, manage costs, and improve outcomes at scale.

About Foster Health

Foster Health is a multidisciplinary team of technologists, strategists, and healthcare professionals united by a shared mission: to improve healthcare through responsible innovation.

We believe that artificial intelligence, when applied thoughtfully, can make healthcare more understandable, more efficient, and more human.
