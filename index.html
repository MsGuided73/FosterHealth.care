Intelligent Technology for Better Healthcare

Foster Health develops AI-driven healthcare technologies that transform complex health information into greater understanding, better-informed decisions, and more effective healthcare.

Our platforms are designed to support—not replace—human expertise, turning complex healthcare data into meaningful insights that help patients, providers, organizations, and researchers make better-informed decisions.

Our Solutions

01 / 04

For Patients

We empower patients with intelligent tools that make complex health information easier to understand and use.

From interpreting medical reports and lab results to providing greater clarity around diagnoses, medications, and treatment options, Foster Health helps individuals navigate healthcare with greater confidence and take a more active role in their care—in collaboration with their healthcare providers.

02 / 04

For Providers

Foster Health supports physicians and healthcare professionals with AI-enabled tools that streamline workflows and enhance clinical decision-making.

By synthesizing patient data and surfacing relevant insights, our solutions help providers improve patient interactions, optimize practice operations, and deliver more efficient, informed care—without adding administrative burden.

03 / 04

For Organizations

We help employers, healthcare organizations, and public-sector entities better understand and manage population health.

Our AI-powered platforms identify meaningful patterns and trends across workforce and community health data, helping organizations develop more effective wellness initiatives, manage healthcare costs, allocate resources, and improve outcomes at scale.

04 / 04

For Research and Clinical Trials

Foster Health develops advanced data collection and analysis platforms designed to help researchers better measure and understand the real-world effects of medications, treatments, and medical interventions.

By integrating patient-reported feedback with clinical data and outcomes, our platforms can provide researchers with greater scope, detail, and flexibility—creating a more comprehensive view of how treatments perform across different patients and populations.

About Foster Health

Foster Health is a multidisciplinary team of technologists, strategists, and healthcare professionals united by a shared mission: to improve healthcare through responsible innovation.

We believe artificial intelligence, when applied thoughtfully and responsibly, can make healthcare more understandable, more efficient, and ultimately more human.
